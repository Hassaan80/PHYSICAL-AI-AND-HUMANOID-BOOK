# APA Citation Guidelines (7th Edition)

This document provides a summary of the American Psychological Association (APA) 7th edition citation style.

## 1. In-Text Citations

In-text citations use an author-date system.

*   **Basic Format**: (Author, Year)
    *   Example: (Smith, 2020)
*   **Direct Quote**: Include a page number.
    *   Example: (Smith, 2020, p. 170)
*   **Multiple Authors**:
    *   Two authors: (Smith & Jones, 2021)
    *   Three or more authors: (Smith et al., 2022)
*   **No Author**: Use the first few words of the title.
    *   Example: ("Understanding APA," 2019)

## 2. Reference List

The reference list is at the end of the document, alphabetized by the author's last name.

*   **Formatting**:
    *   The title "References" is centered and bold.
    *   Use a hanging indent for each entry.
    *   The entire list should be double-spaced.

*   **Common Formats**:

    *   **Journal Article**:
        Author, A. A., Author, B. B., & Author, C. C. (Year). Title of article. *Title of Periodical, volume*(issue), pages. https://doi.org/xxxx

    *   **Book**:
        Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of work* (7th ed.). Publisher.

    *   **Webpage**:
        Author, A. A. (Year, Month Day). *Title of work*. Site Name. URL

## 3. Key Principles

*   **Clarity**: Write clearly and concisely.
*   **Inclusivity**: Use bias-free language.
*   **Discovery**: Help readers find your sources.
